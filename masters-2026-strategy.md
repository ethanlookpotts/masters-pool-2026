## Course analysis: Augusta National

- **Specs:** 7,565 yards, Par 72.
- **Iron play:** Precision is required to hold firm greens and avoid difficult putting areas. Approach performance is the primary differentiator.
- **Par-5s:** Holes 2, 8, 13, and 15 are the primary scoring opportunities and are decisive for the leaderboard.
- **Putting:** Fast surfaces (Stimpmeter 13–14) amplify small errors in judgment or execution.
